# P vs NP Research Project

**Archimedes AI Research Initiative**
**Date:** July 29, 2026

## Mission Statement

This research explores the fundamental question of whether P = NP, using rigorous mathematical methods, computational experiments, and novel theoretical directions. 

**Mantra:** "Se não podemos resolver, que ao menos entendamos por que não podemos."

## Research Areas

1. **Literature Review** - Understanding known impossibility results
2. **Algebraic Structure** - Seeking common patterns across NP-complete problems
3. **Second-Order Logic** - Exploring expressiveness barriers
4. **Game Theory** - Strategic formulations and equilibria
5. **Computational Experiments** - Testing conjectures on small instances
6. **Proof Attempts** - Formal verification attempts
